Sketch Board Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Information We Use

When you sign in with Google, Sketch Board uses your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image to identify your account and connect your board data to your cloud save.

How Data Is Stored

Sketch Board stores your projects, whiteboard content, and custom shape library in your browser. If you enable cloud save, that same board data is stored in private cloud storage associated with your Google account.

How Data Is Shared

Sketch Board does not sell your Google user data. Your Google profile information is used for sign-in and account association only.

Contact
